Famous Places in India

India is home to many famous historical monuments and beautiful natural places. Here are some places that every traveller should know about.

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is located in Agra. It is a famous monument made of white marble and is one of India's most popular tourist attractions.

India Gate

India Gate is located in New Delhi. It is a famous war memorial and an important landmark of the capital city.

Red Fort

The Red Fort is a historic fort in Delhi. It was built using red sandstone and is an important part of India's history.

Qutub Minar

Qutub Minar is a famous historical monument in Delhi. It is known for its tall structure and beautiful stone carvings.

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. It is famous for its beautiful pink architecture and many windows.
