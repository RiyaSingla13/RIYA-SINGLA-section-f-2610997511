About Chitkara University

Chitkara University is an institution of higher education in India. It offers students a place to study, develop useful skills and prepare for future opportunities. Students can explore their interests while learning from teachers and working with other students.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a positive learning environment where students can gain knowledge, think creatively and become responsible members of society.

Our Values

  • Learning: We encourage students to keep asking questions and developing their knowledge.
  • Respect: We value kindness, fairness and respect for others.
  • Teamwork: We help students learn how to share ideas and work together.
  • Responsibility: We encourage students to make thoughtful choices and contribute to society.
