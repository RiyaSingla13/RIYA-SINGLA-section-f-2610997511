Tourist Destinations

Explore three popular Indian destinations, each offering a different kind of holiday.

Manali

Enjoy Himalayan mountain views, green valleys, cool weather, sightseeing, and trekking.

Goa

Relax on sandy beaches, try water sports, and experience Goa's coastal food and culture.

Jaipur

Visit historic forts and palaces, explore lively markets, and learn about Rajasthan's royal heritage.
