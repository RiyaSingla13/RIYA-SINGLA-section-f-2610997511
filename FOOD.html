Famous Indian Foods

Indian cuisine is famous for its rich flavours, spices and variety. Different parts of India have their own special dishes. Indian food includes North Indian dishes, South Indian dishes, street food and delicious desserts.

Famous Indian Dishes

  1. Biryani
  2. Masala Dosa
  3. Paneer Tikka
  4. Samosa
  5. Gulab Jamun

Food Categories

  • North Indian
  • South Indian
  • Street Food
  • Desserts

Food Description

Biryani
A flavourful rice dish prepared with spices and vegetables or meat.
Masala Dosa
A crispy South Indian dish made from rice and lentils and served with chutney.
Samosa
A popular crispy snack filled with spicy potatoes and other vegetables.
